# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two visitors, split by page.

1. **Buyers.** Mumbai families upgrading within the city, and NRI buyers abroad. They usually arrive on a phone in the evening, often from a WhatsApp link a sales manager sent after a call. They return on a laptop with a spouse or parent to compare configurations, carpet areas and travel times. Before they book a visit they need to believe three things: the project is real and RERA-registered, the numbers are exact, and the developer is credible.
2. **People checking the group.** Prospective buyers doing due diligence, landowners and societies weighing redevelopment, partners and press. They read About, Leadership and Press, and want lineage, people and coverage, not adjectives.

## Product Purpose

Rubics Realty is the luxury residential arm of Rubics Group, led by founder and MD Suhan Shetty, building in Mumbai's established neighbourhoods. Its first project is BeauMonté Tower C at Sion Circle (MahaRERA P51900050715). Lalbaug comes next.

The site has to turn credibility into qualified enquiries. Each project page is a complete sales microsite. The group pages make the project pages believable.

We measure success in site-visit requests, calls, WhatsApp chats and brochure requests per project, and in organic visibility for project names and "Sion" or "Lalbaug" luxury searches.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed, elevated.

The voice is a tower's own spec sheet written by someone who loves the city: short sentences, exact numbers, then one line that turns ("Seven to a floor. No exceptions."). It never says "luxury redefined".

The emotional goal, in the visitor's words: "These people have thought about every floor, and they're not hiding anything."

## Anti-references

- The generic Mumbai developer site: a full-screen render slider, "Luxury Redefined", gold script fonts, an enquiry popup on load, and an icon grid of amenities.
- The current home page: an abstract manifesto of 12 vision, mission and philosophy sections with AI-looking imagery and no facts.
- Property portals (MagicBricks, 99acres): dense listing grids, filters everywhere, price-led.
- SaaS landing pages: gradient blobs, glass cards, bento grids, big metric tiles.
- Earlier studio work (Star Asiya, Bombino, CEO Rules): no shared layouts, palettes or type.

## Design Principles

1. **Exact beats impressive.** Carpet areas to two decimals, minutes to the station, the RERA number in view. Precision persuades this buyer.
2. **Every floor is labelled.** The site reads like a tower section. Visitors always know where they are and what's on that level.
3. **One step to a visit.** Every project screen is one tap from call, WhatsApp or booking a visit. Nothing pops up uninvited.
4. **Brand pages earn trust, project pages close.** Group pages carry people, lineage and press. Project pages carry facts and the funnel.
5. **Only what's real.** Rubics' own renders, sketches and portraits. Indicative content is labelled. Unconfirmed claims wait for the client.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, so all motion degrades to static. Touch targets are at least 44px. Every interactive component, including the section explorer, is keyboard operable with visible focus. Use logical CSS properties so Hindi or Marathi pages can ship later without rework. Copy is plain English for readers whose first language may differ. Numbers use Indian conventions where money appears (₹, lakh and crore), but this build shows no prices.
